# 🥗 NutriCore

### Smart Food & Nutrition Manager for iOS

**NutriCore** is a native iOS application focused on bringing food inventory, nutrition tracking, meal planning, recipes, and healthy habits into one intelligent experience.

The project is being developed with **Swift and SwiftUI**, with a strong focus on the Apple ecosystem.

## ✨ What NutriCore Does

### 📦 Smart Food Inventory
Track the food you already have and keep an eye on what needs to be used.

### ⏳ Expiry Intelligence
Monitor expiry dates and surface items that are approaching expiry so they can be used before they become waste.

### 🍳 Recipe Intelligence
NutriCore can use available ingredients to surface recipe ideas, helping turn an existing pantry into actual meals.

### 🔥 Nutrition & Calorie Tracking
Track calorie-related data and nutrition information as part of a broader personal health workflow.

### 🥗 Meal Planning
Plan meals around nutrition goals and the food already available.

### 🏋️ Workout & Progress Support
The application is being expanded toward personalized workout recommendations and progress tracking.

### 🔔 Notifications
Support for reminders and timely actions is built into the application architecture.

### 📊 Personalised Dashboard
A central dashboard brings together inventory, nutrition, recipes, and progress-oriented information.

## 🧱 Tech Stack

## 🏗️ Project Structure

The codebase is organized around separate models, engines, services, managers, and SwiftUI views.

Examples include:

- `Inventory.swift` / `InventoryViewModel.swift`
- `Recipe.swift` / `RecipeEngine.swift`
- `MealPlan.swift` / `MealPlannerEngine.swift`
- `Workout.swift` / `WorkoutEngine.swift`
- `ProfileManager.swift`
- `CalorieManager.swift`
- `NotificationManager.swift`
- `ProductLookupService.swift`

This structure is intended to keep product logic separate from the UI while the application continues to grow.
